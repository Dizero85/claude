## 5. Final checklist & certificate

**Before graduation, the student can:**
- ☐ Introduce themselves for 2 minutes
- ☐ Ask for repetition / clarification without panic
- ☐ Check in at an airport and go through security
- ☐ Pass a 5-minute immigration interview (incl. "serious officer")
- ☐ Check in at a hotel and solve a problem
- ☐ Order at a restaurant, make a special request, pay & tip
- ☐ Buy tickets, take transport, ask for and follow directions
- ☐ Explain a health problem to a doctor / pharmacist
- ☐ Report a lost or stolen item
- ☐ Start, keep and end a small-talk conversation
- ☐ Tell a story about the past
- ☐ Give opinions and talk about plans
- ☐ Completed one real-world conversation in English
