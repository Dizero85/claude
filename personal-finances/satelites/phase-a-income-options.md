# Phase A — Income Options (+~US$450/month)

> Created: 2026-10-01 | Goal: ~4–5 hrs/week in the mornings (7–11:30 am Brazil time), paid in USD

## Key finding

Many US interpreting agencies require you to **live in the US** (e.g., Certified Languages International). Being in Brazil cuts out a lot of options — filter for "worldwide" / "offshore OK" before applying.

| 1 | Freelance PT↔EN medical/health translation (Upwork + agencies) | US$20–30/hr for Brazil-based medical translators; agencies quote ~US$0.09–0.20/word | Yes | Flexible hours, AI-assisted drafting + Di's expert review. Build profile around 12+ yrs patient navigation |

## Math for Phase A

US$450/month ÷ US$25/hr ≈ 18 hrs/month ≈ **4–5 hrs/week**
