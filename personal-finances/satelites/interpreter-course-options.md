# 40-Hour Medical Interpreter Training — Options

> Created: 2026-10-01 | All online, can be done from Brazil. Prices are what providers listed in 2026 — confirm on their site before paying.

## Why this course

Most remote medical interpreting companies require a 40-hour training certificate. It is also the prerequisite for the national certifications (CCHI's CHI and NBCMI's CMI), which unlock better pay.

## Recommendation

- **Tight budget:** MLT (~US$289) — only after confirming CCHI/NBCMI acceptance
- **Best balance:** MITS (~US$640) — clearly approved, 1 year to finish at your own pace

## Language pair choice

Di is trilingual (EN/PT/ES). Spanish interpreting has far more demand than Portuguese in the US. Train for both if the course allows.

## How to verify approval

CCHI publishes its list of approved online programs: https://cchicertification.org/prerequisite-programs/online/
