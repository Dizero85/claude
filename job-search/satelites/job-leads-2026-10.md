## Big caveat — living in Brazil

Most U.S. healthcare employers say "remote **within the United States**". Being a U.S. citizen helps, but many still require you to live in the U.S. (payroll, taxes, patient-data rules). Before applying, look for: "U.S. only", "must reside in", or a list of states. If unclear, ask the recruiter directly: *"I'm a U.S. citizen currently based in Brazil — is that eligible?"*

Roles marked 🌎 accept Brazil / Latin America.
