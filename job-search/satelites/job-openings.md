## Tips for Applying from Brazil

- Filter for **"Worldwide" or "Anywhere"** — some "remote" roles are US-only with state restrictions
- Look for orgs that explicitly state **"work from anywhere"** or have existing international hires
- Medicaid/state-funded roles often require US residency — prioritize **private/nonprofit health orgs**
- Bilingual (English/Portuguese) is a strong differentiator — mention in cover letter
