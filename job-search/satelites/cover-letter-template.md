## Tips for each application

- **Always name the organization** in the last paragraph — never send a generic letter
- **One specific detail** about why you want THIS job at THIS org goes a long way
- **Subject line for email applications:** "Application: [Job Title] — Trilingual Community Engagement Professional"
- **If applying in Spanish:** translate the whole letter — it signals fluency immediately
- **Keep it under 250 words** — hiring managers read fast
